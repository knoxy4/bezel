## 1. Fest and Steam facts

- **Dates:** January 14–18, 2027. Steamworks describes the fest as "games that sit on your desktop and keep you company while you do other things."
- **Who can enter:** themed fests prioritize discounted games, but Coming Soon titles and games with demos are welcome. BEZEL enters as **Coming Soon**.
- **What entry requires:** a real Steamworks app with a public store page. Opt-in happens through a partner link for that app (`https://partner.steamgames.com/optin/sale/desktop_companion_2027`), and invitations go by **store tags**, which must be truthful and match the theme. **The generic test app id can't enter.**
- **Steam Direct timing:**
  - Onboarding takes 1–5 days.
  - The fee is $100 per app, and Valve refunds it once the game has sold $1,000.
  - Release can't happen until 30 days after paying the fee.
  - A public Coming Soon page has to be up for at least two weeks before release.
  - **Upside of paying in December:** by the time BEZEL is shippable, both release clocks have long since run out, so launch can follow soon after Knox says "ship."
- **Overlap:** the Steam Winter Sale runs December 17 to January 4. Review queues may be slower then [Probably].
- **This isn't Next Fest.** A themed fest doesn't spend BEZEL's one Next Fest slot [Probably]. Spec recommends saving Next Fest for **June 2027**, when there's a full demo.

## 4. Gates

**Trailer-ready gate (end of W4, target December 6).** Knox pays the fee only if all of these are true:
- S9 has passed, or the topmost fallback is in place and looks right.
- The loop runs on THOR's real desktop: miners hook a window, a rig and ropes go up, a drag crushes and salvage comes home, and typing heats the foundry.
- Hero sprites come from the locked style frame.
- 30 seconds of footage could be captured that day without anyone being embarrassed.

If the gate slips by up to a week, the plan still holds; it eats W7's buffer. If it slips past December 13, the page lands during the Winter Sale review crunch. Spec calls a fest go/no-go at that point (§6).

**Demo gate (January 4).** The demo ships only if every item passes:
- A 2-hour soak on THOR during real work: no crash, no leaked AppBar, no stuck underlay.
- It recovers from an Explorer restart, a wallpaper change and a virtual-desktop switch.
- On exit, the wallpaper and work area are exactly as they were, including on the live-wallpaper monitor.
- The first-run card discloses pulse counting; turning it off takes one click; the tray has pause and off.
- The see-panel shows live counts, window rectangles and wallpaper paths.
- Stand-down is verified against at least one fullscreen game.
- Uninstall is clean: no AppBar, no underlay window, no autostart entry left behind.

If any item fails, there's no demo and BEZEL enters as Coming Soon only. That's still a full entry.

## 6. Risks

| Risk | Effect | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Trailer-ready gate slips past Dec 13 | Page review lands in the Winter Sale crunch; fest at risk | Go/no-go call on Dec 13. Below the bar → skip this fest and aim for a later one; no fee spent. |
| Onboarding takes the full 5 days or more | App created late | Pre-stage the paperwork in W1, so paying the fee is the only step left at the gate |
| Opt-in deadline is earlier in January than expected | Missed opt-in | Look up the exact date the day the app exists (W5) |
| S9 fails | No underlay | Rev-2 topmost canopy fallback. The trailer still shows grapple, snap and crush on floating windows. |
| Only transparent mode fails | Live-wallpaper monitors lose the frontier | Exclude them by default (D38). Shoot the trailer on a static-wallpaper monitor. |
| Store page rejected | Days lost | Submit early in W6; W7 is buffer |
| AI art inconsistency | Off-model trailer | Lock the style frame in W2; one pipeline configuration for all hero sprites |
| Holidays | W7–W8 capacity drops | Those weeks are buffer and hardening by design |
| Scope creep | Everything slips | Anything outside §2 waits. Spec flags creep on sight. |
