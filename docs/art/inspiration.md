# Art inspiration

## 01: "the whole game in one frame" (Knox, 2026-11-07)

Knox's reference image for BEZEL's look and layout. **Original: `assets/art/reference/inspiration-01.jpg`**
(Gemini-generated concept, saved 2026-11-07). What it shows, and what to take from it:

**SHAFT (left bar):**
- Rough stone walls framing a vertical shaft with steel girders and a pipe running down the inner wall.
- An **elevator cage** with a lamp near the top, and a big **spiral drill bit** under it.
- Mid-shaft: a **battle**: miners with guns on the left wall firing across at green goblins and pink beasts on the right (post-fest shaft battle, D31-adjacent).
- Bottom: an elevator car at trench level with two miners in it.

**The target window (a code editor, generic):**
- A **steel girder frame** sits on the window's top edge, wider than the window, with **round pulley brackets at both top corners**.
- **Cyan cables** run from those corner pulleys straight down both sides of the window to the trench: this is the davit/rope idea from Knox's run-5 note, drawn as a rail system.
- On top: an **outpost**: a shack with a smoking chimney, glowing **cyan crystals** being mined, miners with tools and rifles, crates, a fire.
- A **cable clamp** halfway down the right side of the frame.

**Frontier (bare desktop):**
- Miners **parachuting** down, some holding glowing cyan crystals; a lone crystal falling.

**TRENCH (bottom bar):**
- A rail track and conduit along its top edge (the seam with the desktop).
- Left to right: an elevator car, a **mining machine with a crane arm**, the **foundry**: a brick furnace with a roaring fire and miners working it, stacked crates and barrels, then a **terrarium** of glowing alien plants (teal, purple, a green eye-pod), then scaffolding and ladders.
- Earth and roots under the floor; small grass tufts.

**Palette:** deep slate-blue night desktop; warm orange-amber fire and lamps; **cyan as the signature accent** (crystals, cables, window highlight); earthy browns and greys for stone, timber and steel. Crisp pixel-art sprites, chunky outlines, small readable silhouettes.

**Takeaways for the build:**
1. Cyan crystals are the thing being mined; scrap/salvage can read as crystal shards.
2. Rope davits → corner pulleys + cable rails; the top-edge structure is a girder frame spanning the whole edge, not one box.
3. Trench is a cross-section diorama: rail on top, machines, foundry, terrarium, earth below.
4. Shaft: elevator + drill + wall-to-wall battle lanes (post-fest).
5. Matches style frame 4's palette; use both as `--ref` for hero sprites.
